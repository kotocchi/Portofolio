About Me

Born and raised in Jakarta. Now building a life in Bali. I studied Public Relations, then found my way into visual storytelling. Turning stories nobody's heard into ones an audience actually stops for. That's the throughline in everything I make: take something true and overlooked, and make it land for people who have zero context and even less patience. Translation, just not the language kind.

For the past two years that's meant working fight week itself: Soma Fight Club, and through it, signing announcements and feature content for UFC, ONE Championship, PFL Global, OKTAGON MMA, and Lion FC. I built a video feature on Rabindra Dhant, the first Nepali fighter in Road to UFC, that hit 1.6M+ views without a marketing budget behind it, and that's the piece I'm proudest of, not because of the number, but because nobody asked me to make it. Before this I ran a coffee shop and managed Community Partnerships for a Production House company, which taught me the parts of this work that don't show up in a highlight reel, like managing people, hitting targets, and keeping a process organized under pressure. What I want next is the same instinct, storytelling under real constraints, applied to a bigger canvas than one gym. Brand-side, agency-side, wherever the work is honest and the story's worth telling.
